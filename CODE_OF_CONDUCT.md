# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, safe, and inclusive for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus discussion on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy and never share credentials or private customer data.
- Help maintain a welcoming environment for contributors of all experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting project discussions or development.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through [Screpy Support](https://support.screpy.com/). Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
