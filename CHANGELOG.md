## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-29

### Added

- Custom Screpy REST API-key authentication.
- Twenty actions covering account, project, crawl, crawl-data, comparison, and health operations.
- Find Project, Find Crawl, and Find Page searches.
- New Crawl and Crawl Completed polling triggers.
- Cursor pagination, dynamic project and crawl choices, and structured crawl-data filters.
- Mocked test suite, CI workflow, and Zapier review collateral.
